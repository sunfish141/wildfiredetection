GETTING STARTED

Follow a possible fire.

  1. Place one or more fires at your chosen intensity, or load current FIRMS observations across North America or the visible map area.
  2. Press Play to advance in 12-hour steps with no time limit. Change playback speed, pause, or take a single step.
  3. Click a cell to pause and inspect it. The timeline retains the latest 128 steps; the current state keeps the complete burned-cell mask.

Each point represents a 1 km² cell. Intensity is a scenario scale, not fire radiative power. The research model uses satellite-like fire state and terrain; persistence is heuristic and weather is not included. It is not an operational fire forecast.
